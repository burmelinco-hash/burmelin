Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how BURMELIN ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website, shop at our Bangkok locations, or place orders via our official social media and messaging channels. By engaging with our brand across any platform, you agree to the practices described below.

01

Information We Collect

To provide you with a seamless luxury shopping experience, we collect only the information necessary to fulfill your orders and keep you informed across all platforms.

  • Order Information — When ordering via our website or official social media channels (LINE, WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook), we collect your name, phone number, and delivery address to process your transaction.
  • Contact Details — Your email address is collected if you choose to subscribe for first access to new arrivals and exclusive drops. Subscription is entirely voluntary.
  • In-Store Information — Information you provide during visits to our Bangkok store locations for exchange or membership purposes.
  • Technical Data — Basic usage data such as page visits may be collected automatically via our website host to help us improve your browsing experience. No personally identifiable information is collected without your direct input.
02

How We Use Your Data

  • Order Fulfillment — To process your payment and deliver your apparel to Bangkok and beyond.
  • Customer Support — To assist you with product inquiries, sizing guidance, or our 7-day exchange policy through whichever channel you contact us.
  • Marketing Communication — To send you updates on new collections and exclusive drops, only if you have opted in. You may opt out at any time by messaging us directly on any of our official channels.
  • Service Improvement — To better understand how customers interact with our website and improve your overall experience.
03

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third parties. Your data is only shared in the following limited circumstances:

  • Logistics Partners — Trusted delivery partners receive only the information required to fulfill and ship your order.
  • Third-Party Services — Our website uses external services including Cloudinary (image hosting) and Google Maps (store locations). These services operate under their own privacy policies.
  • Legal Requirements — We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests by public authorities.
04

Data Security

We take reasonable and appropriate steps to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction across all digital interactions. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by applicable law.

05

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. You may also withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through any of the official channels listed below.

06

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Questions about this policy?

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us via any of our official social media channels or via WhatsApp at +66 83 539 8811. We are happy to help.
